About Fruitector

Discover our purpose, our future goals, the minds behind the AI, and how to reach us.

Our Mission

To revolutionize agricultural quality control by providing accessible, AI-driven fruit analysis tools. We strive to reduce global food waste by empowering farmers, distributors, and everyday consumers to identify defects, ripeness, and rot with absolute precision using just their everyday devices.

Our Vision

To become the global standard in mobile and desktop fruit quality detection. We envision a future where seamless data integration—like our automated Excel exports—creates transparent, efficient supply chains, ensuring that only the highest quality produce reaches the table.
